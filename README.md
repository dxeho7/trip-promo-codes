Trip.com does run promotional codes, but they are rarely universal codes that work on every booking. Most offers are limited by product type, user status, country or currency, booking channel, travel dates, and available inventory. A code that works for a first flight may not work for a hotel, train ticket, attraction, or car rental.

The practical approach is simple: check the promotion, confirm that your booking qualifies, and compare the final price after taxes and fees. Travel discounts are useful, but they are not magic. A “50% off” banner attached to a selected hotel is less helpful if the eligible room is unavailable for your dates.

## How to use the Trip.com flight promo code

For an eligible first booking, the usual process is:

1. Open Trip.com and sign in or create a new account.
2. Search for your flight and select the itinerary you want.
3. Continue to the passenger and payment page.
4. Find the **Promo Code**, **Coupon**, or **Promotions** field.
5. Enter `TRIPFLIGHT26`.
6. Apply the code before completing payment.
7. Confirm that the discount appears in the total price.

Trip.com’s flight-deal guidance says that only one flight promo code can normally be applied to a flight order. It also lists common reasons a code may fail: an expired offer, an excluded airline or route, an ineligible fare type, a minimum-spend requirement, a new-user restriction, an app-only condition, or previous use of the code.

That last point matters. A first-order code is generally tied to the account, not just the email address typed into the booking form. Creating multiple accounts to repeat a new-user promotion may violate the platform’s terms and can also create problems if a booking needs to be changed or refunded.

### What to check before applying a flight code

Flight coupons are usually less flexible than they look in a headline. Check these details:

- Whether the booking is your first eligible Trip.com order
- Whether the code applies to the selected departure and arrival markets
- Whether the airline or fare class is excluded
- Whether the discount applies to the base fare only or to the total
- Whether the booking must be completed in the app
- Whether the fare is priced in an eligible currency
- Whether the discount has a maximum value
- Whether the code can be combined with Trip Coins or another coupon
- Whether cancellation or rebooking affects the coupon

Even when the code is accepted, the discount may not reduce taxes, airport charges, baggage fees, seat selection, or other optional services. The important number is the final amount due, not the size of the promotional label.

## Is there a Trip.com hotel promo code?

Yes, but Trip.com’s current hotel promotions are often presented as **claimable coupons** rather than a single public code.

The global promotion page lists a **new-user hotel coupon of up to 10% off**, with Trip.com stating that the offer can be claimed and used in the app. The same page specifically notes that the promotion is available when payment is made in **USD, SGD, or THB**, subject to the offer’s conditions.

The process normally looks like this:

1. Sign in to the Trip.com app or website.
2. Open the promo-code or coupon section.
3. Claim the new-user hotel offer.
4. Tap **Use** or search for an eligible hotel.
5. Select a room that accepts the coupon.
6. Confirm that the discount appears before payment.

Hotel coupons may be excluded from some room types, pay-at-hotel rates, long-stay offers, packages, or bookings supplied by selected partners. The coupon may also have a cap, which means “10% off” does not necessarily mean 10% off an unlimited booking value.

For example, a 10% coupon capped at US$20 is still useful on a US$200 eligible hotel booking, but it will not produce US$100 in savings on a US$1,000 stay. The cap should be visible in the coupon terms before payment.

### How to get a lower hotel total

The most reliable hotel-saving workflow is:

- Search the same hotel with the same dates and room conditions.
- Compare refundable and non-refundable rates separately.
- Check whether breakfast is included.
- Look at the final total, including taxes and service charges.
- Claim the Trip.com coupon before selecting the final payment method.
- Compare the app price with the website price.
- Check whether member pricing or Trip Coins changes the total.

A cheaper headline rate can become less attractive when it excludes breakfast, has stricter cancellation rules, or requires immediate payment. A slightly higher refundable rate may be more sensible for an itinerary that could change.

## Trip.com products and how pricing works

Trip.com does not operate like a software service with a simple monthly pricing table. It is a travel marketplace, so prices depend on dates, destinations, suppliers, availability, fare rules, room types, and the selected currency.

The main product categories currently shown across Trip.com’s official pages include flights, hotels and homes, flight-plus-hotel bookings, trains, car rentals, attractions and tours, airport transfers, eSIM products, cruises, group tours, private tours, and related travel services.

| Trip.com product | How the price is calculated | What can change the final price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flights | Fare selected from an airline or travel supplier | Route, dates, cabin, baggage, fare rules, seat selection, taxes |
| Hotels and homes | Nightly rate multiplied by stay length | Room type, cancellation terms, occupancy, taxes, breakfast |
| Flight + hotel | Combined itinerary quote | Flight inventory, hotel availability, package rules |
| Trains | Route and seat or class selected | Train, departure time, seat class, booking rules |
| Car rentals | Rental quote for vehicle and dates | Supplier, insurance, deposit, mileage, pickup location |
| Attractions and tours | Ticket or tour price per person or booking | Date, ticket type, participant age, availability |
| Airport transfers | Vehicle and route quote | Pickup time, vehicle size, destination, passenger count |
| eSIM | Data package and validity period | Destination, data allowance, package duration |
| Cruises | Cabin and itinerary price | Sailing date, cabin category, port fees, package inclusions |
| Group tours | Published or customized tour price | Destination, group size, inclusions, departure date |
| Private tours | Quote based on itinerary and group details | Guide, vehicle, duration, group size, activities |
| Gift cards | Denomination and regional availability | Country, currency, redemption terms |

Because these are dynamic travel products, there is no honest single “Trip.com package price” that applies to every customer. A flight from New York to Tokyo, a hotel in London, and a train ticket in China each use different inventory and pricing rules.

That also means a coupon should be judged against the complete booking total. A discount can be real and still fail to make the booking cheaper than another available fare.

Trip.com’s rewards program can provide a second layer of savings, although it is separate from a promo code.

Trip Coins can be earned through eligible bookings, hotel reviews, community activity, and promotions. Trip.com states that members can use Trip Coins toward eligible prepaid bookings, and its loyalty information gives the general reference that **100 Trip Coins are approximately equal to US$1**, although the exact value can vary by currency and booking conditions.

Trip.com currently shows several membership tiers, including Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Diamond+, and an invite-only Black Diamond level. Higher tiers can earn more Trip Coins and may include additional travel benefits such as selected lounge access, eSIM packages, airport-transfer upgrades, train refund-fee waivers, or priority support. Benefits and earning rates can change, so they should be checked in the account before planning around them.

Some useful details from Trip.com’s current loyalty information include:

- Silver begins after registration.
- Gold requires one eligible booking.
- Higher tiers require additional bookings or spending.
- Trip Coins are calculated after discounts and promo codes are applied.
- Some bookings may not qualify for Trip Coins.
- Unverified email accounts may be unable to redeem Trip Coins or join related promotions.
- Refunds can affect the return of used Trip Coins according to the booking’s cancellation policy.

The sensible order is to apply a valid coupon first, then see whether Trip Coins can be used on the remaining eligible amount. Do not assume that every promo code and every reward will stack.

## Why a Trip.com promo code may not work

A failed code does not always mean the code is fake. Travel promotions are often conditional.

The most common causes are:

### You are not a new user

A first-order coupon may require a new Trip.com account and no previous eligible purchase. Logging out and trying another browser does not normally change the account’s eligibility.

### The booking is outside the promotion

The coupon may be limited to hotels, flights, attractions, or trains. A hotel coupon will not necessarily apply to a flight-plus-hotel package.

### The selected fare or room is excluded

Promotions can exclude certain airlines, room types, suppliers, routes, or rate plans. The offer may work on one search result and disappear when you select a different fare.

### The app is required

Some coupons are issued or redeemed only through the Trip.com app. If the website does not show the promotion, check the app while signed in to the same account.

### The currency is not eligible

Trip.com’s hotel promotion page specifically references USD, SGD, and THB for the listed new-user hotel offer. A promotion shown in one region may not be valid after switching currencies or locations.

### The promotion has reached its limit

Some campaigns are limited by stock, daily quota, or available inventory. A code can be valid in principle but unavailable when you try to redeem it.

### The offer has conflicting regional dates

This is particularly relevant for car rentals. When two Trip.com pages show different end dates, use the page intended for your region and the terms displayed in checkout. Do not rely on an older global listing.
